## Modular kitchen structure – basic samajhna zaroori hai

Modular kitchen ko broadly 3 parts mein divide kiya ja sakta hai:

1. **Carcass / Box** – Jismein storage hota hai (shelves, drawers, corner units)  
2. **Shutters / Fronts** – Jo bahar se dikhte hain (plain, profile, high gloss, acrylic)  
3. **Countertop & skirting** – Granite, quartz ya solid surface, plus base support

Plywood ka main use **carcass** aur kuch cases mein **shutters** mein hota hai. Agar aap right grade aur thickness choose karte hain to kitchen 10–15 saal easily chal sakta hai, warna 3–4 saal mein edges phoolna, laminate uthna aur hinges loose hona start ho sakta hai.

## BWR vs BWP plywood – clear difference

### BWR – Boiling Water Resistant

- **Standard**: IS:303  
- **Use case**: Normal kitchens, where direct water exposure limited ho  
- **Resistance**: Moisture aur occasional water splashes ko handle kar leta hai  
- **Price**: BWP se sasta, value‑for‑money option  

### BWP – Boiling Water Proof

- **Standard**: IS:710 (Marine grade)  
- **Use case**: High‑moisture areas, sink units, utility, dish‑washer zones  
- **Resistance**: Long boiling water test pass karta hai, glue line zyada strong hoti hai  
- **Price**: BWR se mehenga, lekin life bhi longer hoti hai  

**Simple rule**:  

- **Normal cabinets** → Achha branded **BWR plywood** kaafi hai  
- **Sink ke niche, utility aur wet zone** → Yaha **BWP plywood** strongly recommended hai

## Ideal plywood thickness for modular kitchen

Thickness wrong choose karne se sagging, bending aur joint failure common problems ban jaati hain. Yaha ek practical thumb rule diya gaya hai:

- **18mm plywood** – Carcass sides, shelves, sink unit, tall units ke liye best  
- **12mm / 15mm plywood** – Internal partitions, some light‑duty shelves  
- **6mm / 9mm plywood** – Back panels, shutter inner liners  

Most Delhi modular kitchens mein **18mm** ek industry standard hai. Agar aap MDF or HDMR front use kar rahe hain to bhi backside box 18mm plywood ka hi rakhein.

## BWR vs BWP – kaun sa kahaan use karein?

- **Sink unit, utility area, washing machine base** – **18mm BWP (IS:710)** Meranti plywood  
- **Hob ke aas‑paas ka cabinet** – Achha BWR bhi chalta hai, lekin BWP better rahega  
- **Tall pantry unit & fridge side panels** – 18mm BWR plywood enough hai

## FAQ – Modular kitchen plywood in Delhi

### Q. Sirf MR plywood se kitchen bana sakte hain?

Technically possible hai, lekin Delhi jaise climate mein MR board kitchen ke liye recommended nahi hai. Moisture, steam aur daily cleaning ke wajah se MR board jaldi weak pad jata hai. Minimum **BWR** grade use karna chahiye.

### Q. 16mm plywood chalega kya?

Light usage kitchens mein 16mm bhi use hota hai, lekin **18mm plywood** zyada stable aur long‑term reliable hota hai. Especially drawers aur wide shelves ke liye 18mm hi choose karein.
