## WPC ka matlab kya hai?

**WPC = Wood Plastic Composite.** Ye board lakdi ke powder aur plastic (PVC) ko mila kar banta hai. Isliye isme lakdi jaisa kaam ho jaata hai – cutting, screw, laminate – lekin plastic ki tarah **paani nahi sokhta**.

Iske saath market mein **PVC board** bhi milta hai jo aur bhi halka hota hai, low-load panels ke liye.

## WPC board ke fayde

- **100% waterproof** – paani mein bhi phoolta nahi
- **Termite-proof** – deemak nahi lagti
- **Seelan wali deewar** ke paas bhi kharab nahi hota
- Normal carpentry tools se cut aur fix ho jaata hai
- Upar **laminate** aur **edge band** lag jaata hai

## Kahan use karein?

- **Bathroom vanity** aur wash-basin cabinet
- **Kitchen sink ka neeche wala cabinet** – jahan pipe leak ka dar rehta hai
- **Balcony / utility area** ki storage
- **Ground floor** ke kamre jahan seelan aati hai
- **Bathroom doors** – WPC door kabhi neeche se phoolta nahi

## WPC vs BWP plywood

| Point | WPC Board | BWP Plywood |
|---|---|---|
| Paani | Bilkul nahi sokhta | Jhelta hai, lekin lambe samay tak paani mein rehne par dikkat ho sakti hai |
| Deemak | Nahi lagti | Treatment par depend karta hai |
| Strength / load | Medium | Zyada – heavy furniture ke liye better |
| Screw holding | Theek, sahi screw zaroori | Bahut achha |
| Best for | Bathroom, sink cabinet | Kitchen carcass, heavy shelves |

**Simple rule:** jahan paani roz lagta hai – WPC. Jahan load zyada hai aur nami kabhi-kabhi – BWP plywood.

## Lagate waqt dhyan rakhein

1. **Pre-drilling** karein aur WPC ke liye suggested screws use karein – seedha thokne se screw dheela reh sakta hai.
2. Laminate chipkane ke liye **suitable adhesive** use karein – dukaan par pooch lijiye.
3. Bahut heavy load wali lambi shelf ho toh beech mein support dein.
4. Direct dhoop wali jagah par laminate/finish lagana better hai.

## Kya WPC plywood se mehenga hai?

Rate thickness aur brand par depend karta hai. Lekin bathroom aur sink cabinet mein WPC ki **life zyada** hoti hai – baar-baar repair ka kharcha bach jaata hai. Isliye total cost aksar kam padti hai.
