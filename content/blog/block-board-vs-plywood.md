## Dono dikhte same hain, bante alag hain

Dukaan par 19mm plywood aur 19mm block board saath rakh do toh upar se dono ek jaise lagte hain. Farak andar hai:

- **Plywood** – patli wooden layers (veneers) ko ek-dusre ke cross direction mein chipka kar banta hai. Har direction mein strength barabar rehti hai.
- **Block board** – beech mein **solid lakdi ki strips (blocks)** hoti hain, aur upar-neeche veneer. Strips ek hi direction mein chalti hain.

Isi wajah se dono ki khoobiyan alag hain.

## Block board kab best hai?

Block board ki sabse badi khoobi – **lambi length par bhi seedha rehta hai, sag nahi karta.**

- **Lambi shelves aur bookcase** (3 feet se lambi)
- **Bed ke panels aur headboard**
- **Table top, counter, study table**
- **Doors aur partitions**

Jahan plywood ki same thickness beech se thodi jhuk sakti hai, wahan block board seedha rehta hai – aur aksar thoda **halka** bhi hota hai.

👉 [Block board ke options dekhein](/products/block-board) – 19mm, 22mm aur 25mm.

## Plywood kab best hai?

- **Kitchen cabinets** – khaaskar sink ke paas (BWR/BWP grade)
- **Bathroom vanity** aur geeli jagah
- **Wardrobe carcass** jahan har taraf screw aur hinges lagte hain
- **Chhote pieces** – drawers, frames, small boxes

Plywood **har direction mein screw achhe se pakadta** hai, aur BWR/BWP grade mein nami bhi jhel leta hai. Block board ka solid wood core paani lagne par phool sakta hai.

👉 Kitchen ke liye [BWR plywood](/products/bwr-plywood), sink/bathroom ke liye [BWP plywood](/products/bwp-marine-plywood).

## Side-by-side comparison

| Point | Block Board | Plywood |
|---|---|---|
| Lambi span par seedha | ✅ Bahut achha | Thoda sag ho sakta hai |
| Screw holding | Strip ki direction mein achha | Har direction mein achha |
| Paani / nami | ❌ Avoid karein | ✅ BWR/BWP grade mein achha |
| Weight | Aksar halka | Thoda bhari |
| Best use | Shelves, bed, table, doors | Kitchen, bathroom, wardrobe carcass |

## Carpenter ke saath planning tip

Block board mein screws **strips ki direction** mein zyada achhe pakadte hain. Isliye shelves aur panels cut karte waqt carpenter ko pehle se bata dein ki kahan hinges aur screws lagenge. Kinare (edges) par hamesha **edge band tape** lagwayein – isse nami andar nahi jaati.

👉 [PVC edge band tape](/products/pvc-edge-band-tape) aapke laminate ke shade mein milta hai.

## Mixed approach sabse practical

Zyaadatar ghar mein best result **dono ko mila kar** aata hai:

- Wardrobe ka dhancha (carcass) – **plywood**
- Lambi shelves aur loft – **block board**
- Kitchen – **BWR/BWP plywood**, shutters ke liye plywood ya [HDHMR](/products/hdhmr-board)

Isse strength bhi milti hai aur budget bhi control mein rehta hai.
