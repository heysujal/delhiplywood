## MDF vs plywood for wardrobes – kaun si cheez kaha use karein?

### Plywood

- Multiple wood veneers (plies) se banta hai  
- Moisture resistance MDF se better hoti hai  
- Screw holding capacity strong hoti hai  
- Long‑term load bearing ke liye perfect  

**Best use:**  
- Wardrobe carcass (box), loft storage, heavy hangers, shelves

### MDF

- Wood fibre + resin se banta hai, uniform & smooth  
- Routing work, grooving, CNC patterns mein perfect  
- Screw holding plywood se thoda kam, but shutter fronts ke liye kaafi  

**Best use:**  
- Wardrobe shutters / doors with stylish grooves  
- Painted or PU finish ke liye  

**Smart combo:**  

> **Carcass (andar wala box)** → Plywood  
> **Shutters (bahar ke darwaze)** → MDF / HDMR + laminate / PU

Delhi Plywood House par hum ye hybrid system hi maximum clients ko recommend karte hain – strength + design dono mil jata hai.

## Recommended thickness – 12mm, 16mm, 19mm kaise choose karein?

### 19mm plywood

- Heavy duty wardrobes, floor‑to‑ceiling units, long shelves ke liye best  
- Master bedroom, main wardrobes, walk‑in closets ke liye ideal  

### 16mm plywood

- Guest room, light storage, smaller depth wardrobes  
- Budget projects jahan load average ho  

### 12mm plywood

- Typically backs, partitions, drawer bottoms ya non‑load‑bearing surfaces ke liye  
- Full carcass ke liye 12mm avoid karein – sagging risk badh jata hai

**Practical recommendation (Delhi homes):**

- Wardrobe sides, top, bottom: **16mm ya 19mm plywood**  
- Shelves: 19mm preferred for long span, 16mm for short shelves  
- Back panel: 6mm–9mm plywood or MDF  
- Shutters: 18/19mm MDF or plywood + laminate

## Interior vs exterior boards – kya difference hai?

- **Interior grade plywood (MR/BWR)**  
  - Bedroom wardrobes ke liye generally enough  
  - Short‑term moisture (AC condensation, cleaning) handle kar leta hai  

- **Exterior grade / BWP plywood**  
  - Very high moisture areas ke liye  
  - Wardrobes jo direct bathroom wall ke saath lag rahe hain, unka back side BWP ya moisture barrier ke saath design karna safe rahta hai

Smell, seepage aur leakage ke chance agar wall par ho, toh zaroor hi contractor se moisture treatment + right grade plywood ki demand karein.

## Hardware tips – good plywood + bad hardware = problem

Wardrobe ka experience sirf board par depend nahi karta, hardware bhi equally important hai:

- **Soft‑close hinges** – heavy shutters ke liye high‑quality hinges zaroor lagwayein  
- **Telescopic / tandem channels** – drawer movement smooth aur long‑lasting hota hai  
- **Locks & handles** – rust‑free, brand certified hardware use karein

## Cost estimates – ek standard wardrobe ke liye approx material

Let’s assume ek **7 ft high x 6 ft wide** wardrobe with loft:

- Carcass plywood sheets (16/19mm) – approximate 5–7 sheets  
- Back panel – 2–3 sheets of 6/9mm  
- Shutters – MDF / plywood sheets 3–4 (design & divisions par depend karta hai)  
- Laminate / sunmica – outside + inside combination  

Overall cost:

- **Budget wardrobe** – Pine plywood + basic laminates  
- **Mid‑range** – Meranti plywood + branded laminates (Greenlam, Merino, Century)  
- **Premium** – BWP plywood strategic areas + designer laminates / PU shutters

## Bilingual Wardrobe FAQ – Hindi + English

### Q1. *Wardrobe ke liye 16mm ya 19mm better hai?* / Which is better – 16mm or 19mm?

**Answer:**  
Light use ke liye 16mm theek hai, lekin agar aap heavy clothes, blankets, extra storage aur long‑term durability chahte hain to **19mm plywood** best rahega. Hum master bedroom wardrobes ke liye 19mm aur guest rooms ke liye 16mm recommend karte hain.

### Q2. *Kya poori wardrobe MDF se bana sakte hain?* / Can I make full wardrobe with MDF?

**Answer:**  
Technically possible hai, lekin **carcass ke liye plywood** stronger aur reliable hota hai. MDF ke carcass me screw holding aur long‑term sagging ka risk zyada hai. Best combo – **plywood carcass + MDF shutters**.

### Q3. *Back side ka board kaunsa lena chahiye?* / Which board for wardrobe back?

**Answer:**  
Normally **6mm ya 9mm plywood / MDF** backs ke liye use hota hai. Agar wall dampness prone hai toh ya toh BWP back panel use karein ya wall treatment karwa kar gap & ventilation plan karein.

### Q4. *Wardrobe ke liye kaun sa laminate acha rahega?* / Which laminate is good for wardrobes?

**Answer:**  
Matt, suede aur light texture laminates wardrobe ke liye best hote hain – scratches kam dikhte hain, fingerprint issues bhi kam rehte hain. Brands jaise **Greenlam, Merino, Century** reliable options hain.
