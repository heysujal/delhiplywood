## Edge band tape kya hota hai?

Plywood, MDF ya HDHMR ko cut karne ke baad uski **side (edge)** kachchi dikhti hai – layers ya fibres khule rehte hain. **Edge band tape** ek patli PVC patti hoti hai jo is edge ko cover karti hai.

Ye sirf sundar dikhne ke liye nahi hai – ye **edge ko seal** karti hai taaki nami andar na jaaye. Kitchen shutters aur MDF/HDHMR mein ye bahut zaroori hai.

## Thickness kaunsi lein?

- **0.8mm** – shutters, shelves aur normal furniture ke liye common
- **1mm – 2mm** – jahan edge par baar-baar takkar lagti hai: kitchen counters ke kinare, table edges, drawers ke front

Moti tape zyada chot jhelti hai aur kinara round finish ke saath achha lagta hai.

## Width kaise chunein?

Tape ki width **board ki thickness se thodi zyada** honi chahiye, taaki lagane ke baad extra trim ho sake:

| Board thickness | Tape width |
|---|---|
| 16–19mm | 22mm |
| 25mm | 30mm ya zyada |

Doubled edges (do boards jod kar) ke liye aur chaudi tape leni padti hai.

## Shade kaise match karein?

1. Laminate ka **sample ya shade number** dukaan par le aayein.
2. **Solid colours, wood grain, marble aur high-gloss** – har finish mein tapes milti hain.
3. Wood-grain laminate ke saath thoda **dark shade** aksar better blend karta hai.
4. Pure project ki tape **ek saath** lein – alag batch mein shade thoda farak ho sakta hai.

## Lagane ke do tarike

- **Edge banding machine** – sabse saaf aur mazboot finish, factories aur bade workshops mein.
- **Haath se (manual)** – carpenter suitable adhesive se lagata hai aur trim karta hai. Ghar ke kaam ke liye bhi achha result deta hai agar surface saaf ho aur adhesive sahi ho.
