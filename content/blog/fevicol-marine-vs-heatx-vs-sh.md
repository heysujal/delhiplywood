## Sahi gond = furniture ki lambi life

Achha plywood aur achha laminate le liya, lekin **galat adhesive** laga diya toh kuch mahino mein laminate uthne lagta hai ya joint khul jaata hai. Fevicol ke har product ka kaam alag hai – ek hi dabba har jagah use karna sabse aam galti hai.

## Fevicol SH – general woodwork

- Normal **wood-to-wood joints** aur dry areas ka kaam
- Bedroom furniture, wardrobe, study table, beds
- Paani wali jagah ke liye nahi

Ye carpenters ka sabse common, everyday gond hai.

## Fevicol Marine – waterproof kaam

- **Kitchen, bathroom** aur jahan paani/nami rehti hai
- Sink cabinet, vanity, balcony furniture
- BWR/BWP plywood ke saath best combination

Agar plywood waterproof liya hai aur gond normal laga diya, toh joint wahi se kharab hoga. **Geeli jagah = Marine.**

👉 Isko [BWP & marine plywood](/products/bwp-marine-plywood) ke saath use karein.

## Fevicol HeatX – garmi wali jagah

- Kitchen mein **gas stove / chimney ke paas** laminate chipkane ke liye
- Jahan garmi se normal gond naram ho sakta hai

Stove ke aas-paas ke shutters aur side panels mein HeatX lagwayein – laminate uthne ka dar kam hota hai.

## Fevicol ProBond – acrylic aur PVC sheets

- **Acrylic sheets** aur **PVC sheets** chipkane ke liye (high-grab adhesive)
- High-gloss acrylic shutters, PVC panels

Normal wood glue acrylic/PVC par achhi pakad nahi deta – iske liye ProBond jaisa special adhesive chahiye.

## Quick table

| Kaam | Kaunsa lein |
|---|---|
| Dry area wood joints | Fevicol SH |
| Kitchen / bathroom / sink | Fevicol Marine |
| Stove ke paas laminate | Fevicol HeatX |
| Acrylic / PVC sheets | Fevicol ProBond |

## Lagane ke tips

1. Surface **saaf aur sookha** ho – dhool aur tel pakad kamzor karte hain.
2. Dono surfaces par **patli, barabar layer** lagayein.
3. Laminate lagane ke baad **press / clamp** zaroor karein aur utna time dein jitna dabbe par likha hai.
4. Dabbe ki **expiry aur instructions** padh lein – har product ka setting time alag hota hai.
