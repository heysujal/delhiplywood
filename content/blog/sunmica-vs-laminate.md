## Sunmica vs laminate – kya asli difference hai?

Roz‑marra ki language mein log **sunmica** aur **laminate** ko same samajh lete hain. Technically, Sunmica ek brand name tha jo itna popular hua ki log har decorative laminate ko hi sunmica kehne lage.  

2025 mein market completely laminate‑driven hai – multiple brands jaise **Greenlam, Merino, Century** high‑pressure laminates (HPL) aur compact laminates banate hain. Lekin homeowner ke level par sawal wahi hai:

- Sunmica/laminate kis thickness mein lena chahiye?  
- Kitchen, wardrobe, TV unit ke liye kaun sa better hai?  
- Durability aur maintenance kis mein easy hai?  
- Delhi jaise climate mein kis cheez ki life zyada hoti hai?  

Is guide mein hum ye sab points clear karenge.

## Sunmica kya tha? Aaj ke time mein uska place kya hai?

Sunmica originally ek **decorative laminate** ka brand tha. Jaise Fevicol adhesive ka brand hai, lekin log har glue ko Fevicol bol dete hain, waise hi laminates ke case mein Sunmica ho gaya.

Aaj ke time mein:

- Market mein **do main categories** popular hain – *decorative laminates* & *acrylic / PU finishes*  
- Decorative laminates mostly **0.8mm–1mm thickness** mein milte hain  
- Ye plywood, MDF ya HDMR ke upar paste kiye jaate hain (with Fevicol SR aur press)  

So jab contractor bolta hai “sunmica lag jayega”, woh actually **laminate** hi refer kar raha hota hai.

## Laminate thickness & grades – kya options milte hain?

Home interiors ke liye common laminate options:

- **0.7mm–0.8mm** – Budget interiors, back side, low‑traffic surfaces  
- **1.0mm** – Standard thickness, zyada durable, daily wear & tear ke liye better  
- **1.25mm+** – Heavy duty / special applications, compact laminates, exterior grade etc.

Nitin Plywood House par hum mainly **0.8mm aur 1mm** range ka **Greenlam, Merino, Century** jaise brands ka stock rakhte hain, jo modular kitchen, wardrobe aur furniture ke liye ideal hain.

## Durability – kaun sa surface kitna strong hai?

### Laminates (Sunmica)

- Scratch resistance: Achhi, especially 1mm laminates  
- Heat resistance: Normal hot utensils se thoda distance rakhna chahiye, but daily kitchen use handle kar lete hain  
- Moisture resistance: Proper bonding & edge banding ke saath good performance  
- Ideal for: Kitchen shutters, wardrobe shutters, TV unit, crockery unit, study tables

### Paint / PU finish

- Looks premium, but scratch marks jaldi dikh jaate hain  
- Maintenance high hota hai, especially glossy PU mein  
- Re‑polishing possible hai, but regular ghar ke liye thoda mehenga solution ho sakta hai  

Isiliye Delhi ke maximum flats aur kothiyon mein **laminate finish** hi favourite choice bani hui hai – budget friendly, durable aur design options bohot zyada.

## Best use cases – kitchen vs wardrobe vs other furniture

### Modular kitchen ke liye

- **Outside shutters**: 1mm laminate / sunmica recommended  
- **Inside surface**: White laminate ya pre‑laminated board, easy cleaning ke liye  
- **Countertop wall side (skirting)**: Dark color laminate stains hide karne ke liye accha rehta hai  

Kitchen ke liye **bohot light glossy acrylic** lena avoid karein agar heavy cooking hoti hai – fingerprints aur scratches jaldi visible hote hain.

### Wardrobe & bedroom furniture

- Wardrobes, lofts, study tables, side tables ke liye **0.8mm ya 1mm laminate** excellent choice hai  
- Matt, suede aur texture finish bedroom mein classy look deti hain  
- Sliding wardrobes ke liye straight grain designs (teak, oak, walnut look) bohot demand mein hain  

### TV unit, crockery & decorative partitions

- Yaha thoda design play kar sakte hain –  
  - High gloss white/cream + woodgrain combination  
  - Fluted panels ke saath solid tone laminates  
  - Glass + laminate mashup

Nitin Plywood House par aap **Greenlam, Merino, Century** jaise brands ke design folders dekh kar live selection kar sakte hain.

## Cost comparison – laminate vs other finishes (Delhi 2025)

Approximate idea ke liye:

- **Standard 1mm laminate (per sq.ft on plywood)**  
  - Material + labour = mid‑range cost  
  - Long‑term value mein best balance  

- **PU / Duco paint on MDF**  
  - Zyada expensive  
  - Re‑touch possible but repeated maintenance required  

- **Acrylic sheets**  
  - Super high gloss finish  
  - Scratch sensitivity zyada, rate bhi high  

Isliye agar aap **value‑for‑money** aur **durability** dono chahte hain, toh laminate / sunmica finish sabse practical solution hai.

## Maintenance tips – sunmica / laminate ko kaise maintain karein?

- Daily cleaning ke liye sirf **soft cloth + mild soap solution** use karein  
- Koi bhi **acidic cleaning chemical** directly laminate par mat lagayein  
- Direct hot tawa ya pan surface par mat rakhein – always use hot pad / trivet  
- Water seepage se bachne ke liye edges aur corners properly sealed hone chahiye  

Proper use se laminate finish 10–15 saal tak easily chal sakti hai.
