## Why plywood prices vary so much in Delhi

If you search for plywood prices in Delhi, you will find very different quotes for what sounds like the same board.
The reason is simple: **grade, core quality, thickness, and brand consistency** change the final cost.

For home projects, always compare these points before comparing price:

- Grade (MR, BWR, BWP/Marine)
- Thickness (6mm, 9mm, 12mm, 16mm, 18mm, 19mm)
- Core quality (voids, patching, face finish)
- Bill and GST details

## Typical use-case pricing logic

Instead of chasing one "lowest rate," buy by use-case:

- **Kitchen carcass:** usually BWR or BWP in 18mm
- **Sink zone/wet areas:** BWP/Marine recommended
- **Wardrobe carcass:** 16mm or 19mm plywood
- **Back panels:** 6mm or 9mm

This approach prevents expensive repair work later.

## Budget vs mid-range vs premium

In most Delhi projects, material choice falls into three bands:

- **Budget:** lower grade or non-standard boards, short-term savings, higher long-term risk
- **Mid-range:** balanced quality and value for regular homes
- **Premium:** best consistency, moisture resistance, and long-term hardware holding

For most families, **mid-range with strategic premium use in wet zones** is the best value.

## Hidden costs people miss

Many quotes exclude supporting items. Ask for these separately:

- Laminate/sunmica thickness and brand
- Adhesive quality
- Edge banding and finishing
- Hardware (hinges, channels, handles)
- Transport and unloading

If these are ignored, the "cheap quote" becomes expensive quickly.

## Smart buying checklist

Before finalizing a purchase:

1. Confirm board grade and thickness in writing.
2. Ask for GST bill and brand details.
3. Check one sample sheet physically.
4. Reserve better grade for wet zones first.
5. Match board selection to hardware quality.
